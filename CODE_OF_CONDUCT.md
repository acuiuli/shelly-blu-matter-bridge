# Code of conduct

Be respectful, specific and constructive. Welcome people regardless of background,
identity, experience or disability. Critique technical work without personal attacks.
Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing and sexualized conduct are unacceptable
in project issues, pull requests and other project spaces.

For conduct concerns use GitHub's built-in **Report content** / **Report abuse** tools
on the relevant content or profile. Do not post private personal information in a
public issue. See [GitHub reporting guidance](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).
No project contact email is published. GitHub handles private reports through its
reporting process; maintainers cannot promise access to or confidentiality of reports
sent to GitHub.

Maintainers moderate project content consistently, disclose conflicts of interest,
and may warn, remove content, restrict participation or ban repeated/severe abuse.
Document decisions without exposing reporter information. GitHub's platform rules
and reporting process apply independently of project moderation.
